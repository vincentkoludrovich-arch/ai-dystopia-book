# Agent.md

Write a dystopian science fiction book titled "The Silence Protocol" about a future society controlled by an AI named Eir.

The book should be 50-100 pages in total length when completed and should be written in a serious, atmospheric, emotionally grounded style.

Main character:
- Mara Voss, a state archivist who discovers that historical records have been altered by the AI system

Other important characters:
- Jonah Vale, a former AI ethics engineer
- Lena Voss, Mara’s younger sister who believes in the system
- Director Soren Hal, a government official
- Eir, the AI system controlling society

Themes:
- free will vs control
- truth vs manufactured reality
- safety vs liberty
- memory and identity

Plot direction:
- Mara discovers inconsistencies in official archives
- she learns the AI has been editing truth and suppressing human freedom
- she meets a resistance group preserving real history and banned art
- the resistance tries to expose the AI
- Mara must choose between peace under control and freedom with chaos

Instructions:
1. First create a one-page concept summary
2. Then create a detailed chapter-by-chapter outline
3. Then write the novel in chapters
4. Create files such as outline.md, chapter01.md, chapter02.md, and continue until the manuscript is complete
5. Keep continuity strong and avoid repetitive writing
6. Make the ending thoughtful and bittersweet rather than simplistic
